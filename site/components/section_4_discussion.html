Fundamental Processing Philosophy Differences

The three analysis centers employ fundamentally different processing philosophies that would be expected to produce different artifacts if the signal were methodological in origin:

  • IGS (Multi-Center Combination): Combines solutions from multiple analysis centers using weighted averaging. This represents a meta-analysis rather than an independent processing approach, though it includes contributions from centers with diverse methodologies.

Critical implication: Network solutions and PPP represent philosophically opposite approaches to GNSS processing. Network solutions depend on inter-station connectivity for their solution, while PPP explicitly avoids it.
