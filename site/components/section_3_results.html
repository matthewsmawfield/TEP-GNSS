The observed patterns exhibit remarkable multi-center consistency and demonstrate sensitivity to Earth's motion, gravitational fields, and temporal structure.

  6. Gravitational coupling: Planetary correlations with timescale strengthening (r = -0.552 at 240 days)
  7. Temporal structure: Diurnal (1.9-7.6% day-night variation) and seasonal modulation
  8. Dynamic responses: Coherence modulations during eclipses and supermoon events

These observations indicate systematic sensitivity of global GNSS networks to large-scale spatial structure, Earth's motion, and gravitational fields. Comprehensive validation demonstrating signal authenticity is presented in Section 4.2.
