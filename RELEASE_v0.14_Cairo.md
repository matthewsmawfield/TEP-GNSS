# v0.14 (Cairo): Multi-Resolution CMB Frame Verification

## Summary

Multi-resolution verification of CMB frame alignment across four independent resolutions (10°, 5°, 2.5°, 1°) spanning 93-fold range in tested directions (703 → 65,341). Results show monotonic convergence (r=0.707→0.747) with coordinate stability within 1-2° (RA: 180°→190°→187°→186°). All resolutions identify CMB frame alignment (separation 18-22°) rejecting Solar Apex (86-89°). The 5,570-fold variance ratio (CMB vs Solar Apex) confirms GNSS clock correlations couple to Earth's motion through the Cosmic Microwave Background rest frame (369 km/s toward RA=168°, Dec=-7°).

Global GNSS clock networks exhibit distance-structured temporal correlations that align with Earth's velocity through the CMB rest frame, tested across 65,341 sky directions. The correlation pattern points consistently toward the same cosmic direction (within 1-2°) across four independent resolution scales, demonstrating robust coupling to our motion through the universe.
